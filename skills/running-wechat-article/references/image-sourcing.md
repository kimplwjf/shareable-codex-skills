# Image Sourcing and Attribution

## Use priority

1. User-owned or user-provided material with stated permission.
2. Organizer, athlete, team, brand, or venue media assets with explicit reuse terms.
3. Open-license material whose license permits the intended commercial/editorial use and modification.
4. Original diagrams, maps, charts, or illustrations made from verified facts.

## Reject by default

- Search-result thumbnails, social-post images, news photos, photographer portfolios, and images with missing reuse terms.
- Assets that display a watermark, a platform mark, a license restriction, or a third-party logo that cannot be reused.
- Any image that would imply sponsorship, endorsement, or official affiliation.
